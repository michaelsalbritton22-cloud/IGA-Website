IGA Tour Rules

The official rules and playing conditions of the Internal Golf Association.

1. League Format

The Internal Golf Association is an individual stroke-play golf league. Players compete throughout the season for points, championships, and course honors.

  • All regular-season events are individual stroke play.
  • Each player's score is recorded as both a gross score and a net score.
  • Net scores are used for league standings and regular-season event results.
  • Each player is allowed one mulligan per round.
  • There is no additional scoring multiplier applied to handicap or event results.

3. Handicap

The IGA uses the player's current 18Birdies handicap when calculating net scores.

  • Handicaps are dynamic and are not locked for the entire season.
  • The handicap used for a round is recorded with that round.
  • Net scores are calculated using the applicable handicap for that round.

4. General Playing Rules

The IGA is designed to keep rounds competitive, enjoyable, and moving.
  1. Stroke Play. All regular IGA rounds are played as individual stroke play.
  2. No Gimmies. Every putt must be holed. Players must putt everything out.
  3. Breakfast Ball. A player may use one breakfast ball on the tee shot of Hole #1 only.
  4. Lift, Clean and Place. Players may lift, clean, and place their ball for each shot.
  5. One Club Length. A player may move the ball up to one club length for each shot, except when putting on the green.

5. Relief

Unplayable Ground Conditions

A player may move the ball more than one club length when the ball would otherwise require a player to hit from an unreasonable or damaging playing surface.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Cart paths
  • Rocks
  • Tree roots
  • Muddy areas

Bunkers With Standing Water

If a bunker contains standing water, the player may move the ball out of the bunker to a reasonable playable location without additional penalty.

6. Water Hazards & Out of Bounds

A ball that enters a water hazard or goes out of bounds results in a one-stroke penalty.

Water Hazard

When a ball enters a water hazard, the player may cross over the water hazard and place the ball on the other side of the hazard.

Out of Bounds

If a tee shot or fairway shot goes out of bounds, the player may place the ball laterally to the location where the ball came to rest.

The applicable one-stroke penalty still applies.

7. Score Submission

Players are responsible for submitting their scores promptly after completing a round.

  • Scores should be submitted in the IGA golf chat within 24 hours of completing the round.
  • Scores may be submitted from 18Birdies or through the IGA's designated scoring process.
  • The Commissioner reviews submitted scores before they become official.
  • Results remain provisional until the round has been approved and finalized.

8. Course Qualification

Players must complete the required qualifying rounds at each designated qualifying course.

  • Westwood Park requires two qualifying rounds.
  • Firelake requires two qualifying rounds.
  • Jimmie Austin requires two qualifying rounds.
Six qualifying rounds are required for complete course qualification.

The qualifying courses are used for the IGA's King of the Course competition.

9. Championship Events

IGA League Championship

The overall IGA League Champion is determined by regular-season league performance and accumulated league points.

King of the Course

Each qualifying course has its own King of the Course. The title is awarded to the player with the lowest combined net score using the required qualifying rounds.

Folger's Redline Classic

The Folger's Redline Classic is the IGA's mid-season team championship and uses Ryder Cup-style competition.

Audit Master

The Audit Master is the IGA's premier individual championship and is held in October.

10. Membership & Administration

  • IGA membership is $20 per player.
  • A 3% administrative fee is applied to league funds.
  • League funds and administrative decisions are managed by the IGA Commissioner.

11. Commissioner Authority

The Commissioner is responsible for administering the league, reviewing submitted scores, maintaining official standings, and resolving questions regarding the application of IGA rules.

The Commissioner may make reasonable determinations when a situation is not specifically addressed by these rules, with the goal of protecting the integrity and competitiveness of the league.
